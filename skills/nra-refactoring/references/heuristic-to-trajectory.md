# Cheap leads → accepted decisions → ordered NRA trajectory

NRA's heuristic output is a **search layer**. It isn't a weaker kind of proof.
`SOURCE_BACKED_CONDITION_LEAD` finds a single `if`/`while` test with four or
more direct `and`/`or` terms. `SOURCE_BACKED_ROSTER_LEAD` finds repeated
literal string containers of at least three values in one module. Sets compare
members; tuples and lists compare original order. Both keep the original
source rows, are `strong_heuristic`, and have **no executable recipe**. They're
a cheap way to pick maintenance questions. They don't say every long condition
is broken or that every matching container is one domain family. The
string-dispatch lead gives you the original case witnesses to go with them.

For each cluster of leads, investigate it yourself instead of asking the owner
to list every case:

1. Pin the revision, the production corpus you picked and the detector
   coverage. Read the original tests, containers and consumers, including rows
   that were skipped or didn't line up. Write down the competing owners and the
   counterexample that would kill a shared owner. Sort each condition term as
   an independent predicate, a repeated fact, a foreign state probe or a case
   selection, **using the source and the domain input**, not how many terms
   there are. A matching roster may just be an external protocol vocabulary,
   and you need to keep that contract.
2. Put one scoped decision in front of the reader for the whole maintenance
   object: the intended answer, the proposed public owner/ABC, the members you
   accept and the capabilities that cross, the cases that stay independently
   owned, the value-only alternative (if you can justify one), and the exact
   OPEN rows. State the new-case edit experiment. A domain rule, or what the
   task says to do, is what makes something a family. Matching literals never
   do.
3. Once the family is accepted, build **one ordered plan for all the dependent
   decisions**: declaration and shared behavior; derived membership; migration
   of each consumer and each guard with effects; deletion of mirrors; a
   terminal forbidden-path guard. Simulate each stage on projected source and
   re-resolve the next selectors. Check the source revision, binding,
   initializer effects, MRO, priority, short-circuit behavior, external
   formats, fallback and async order. A passing preflight doesn't mean
   behavior is equivalent. An OPEN obligation blocks applying the combined
   plan, but it doesn't block exploratory planning.
4. Verify behavior at the real boundary and count the edits for a new case.
   Rerun a full scan at the target revision, and report coverage and any
   evidence you couldn't resolve. The plan isn't done while a second owner
   survives.

That's how cheap searches cut the busywork **before** the real design call.
The practitioner LLM already investigates and chains those decisions. It can
accept one ownership relation, simulate the dependent stages, read the
projected source and make the next call, ending up with a large ordered
codemod. NRA gives you source leads plus a deterministic DSL and proof gates.
It doesn't infer business meaning and it doesn't certify moving arbitrary
async source. Don't mistake this existing LLM-led workflow for an unattended
plan generator.
