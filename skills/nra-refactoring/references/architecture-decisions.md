# Domain decision receipts: theory and source-calibrated cases

Read the maintained `../../../docs/source/development/semantic_derivation_protocol.rst` (especially phases 0–3 and 7), `../../../docs/source/development/nominal_architecture_playbook.rst`, and the current sources before changing architecture. The OOPSLA preprint *Semantic Completeness for Correct Maintenance: A Strict Hierarchy of Typing and Inheritance* motivates a **task-relative** required-answer relation: for bounded domain meanings/states `D` and required questions `Q`, can the chosen representation derive every admitted answer without confusing states with different answers? Matching current values or counting classes cannot establish shared future meaning. Inheritance is one possible mechanism; admitted obligations, lookup and substitution decide whether it fits.

## Agent's decision receipt (reasoning worksheet, not an NRA wire schema)

1. **Boundary and role names:** exact checkout and source/dependency revisions, bounded context, domain noun(s), lifetime/phase, independent future variation and same-shape/different-meaning collisions.
2. **Source witnesses:** original declaration, method, branch, caller, fallback and consumer rows. Distinguish independently writable rosters from maps derived from them; distinguish schema, domain, UI and transport authority. Retain unmatched, dynamic, alias, duplicate/rebound and OPEN rows with the reason each is unresolved.
3. **Candidate `R*`:** provisional required and forbidden implementation–consumer/class pairs, proposed determining authority *per fact family*, needed public parent contract or alternative owner, derived projections, named exceptions and residual boundaries. A nonexistent subtype is not itself a missing obligation.
4. **Decision:** `propose`, `alternative` or `abstain`; cite source evidence, describe why independent maintenance shrinks, and name counterevidence and admission conditions. An LLM can make an evidence-backed bounded-domain judgment when authorized; escalate contested meaning. No judge's confidence, historical diff or repeated spelling establishes behavioral equivalence.
