# Domain decision receipts: theory and source-calibrated cases

Before you change architecture, read the maintained `../../../docs/source/development/semantic_derivation_protocol.rst` (especially phases 0–3 and 7), `../../../docs/source/development/nominal_architecture_playbook.rst`, and the current sources. The OOPSLA preprint *Semantic Completeness for Correct Maintenance: A Strict Hierarchy of Typing and Inheritance* argues for a **task-relative** required-answer relation. For the domain meanings/states `D` and required questions `Q` in scope: can the representation you chose derive every answer the task requires without mashing together states that need different answers? Matching current values or counting classes can't tell you whether two things will mean the same thing later. Inheritance is one possible mechanism. Whether it fits depends on the obligations, lookup and substitution.

## Reusable refactor shapes pulled from the PR transitions

These are **search and plan-shaping moves** the agent applies itself. They are not canned answers for another domain.

| Observed maintenance shape | Candidate ownership move | Check before prescribing |
|---|---|---|
| An existing nominal declaration plus hand-authored names/aliases/policies that have to change together (#60) | Put the fact on its declaring member/class, along with the variant-specific behavior. Derive lookup, exports and adapter projections. Remove the independent roster. | Keep genuinely different owner families apart (setup vs backend), plus aliases, exceptional leaf hooks, unknown inputs and dynamic registration. Derived lookup maps are not mirrors. |
| Repeated concrete-type/`hasattr` choices in UI managers (#44) | Name the actual capability question. Where a public family can satisfy it, put the shared invariant on the nominal contract and the irreducible hook on a substitutable leaf. Keep a framework adapter when that's its own role. | A service that still switches on concrete Qt type is just relocation. Reset/read-only can be separate questions. The old guarded fallback and the new unguarded one differ. |
| State that looks like it lives in a view, with `self.state` forwarding added later (#58) | **If** lifecycle, identity and domain obligations back it up, consider an `ObjectState` owner for the state facts and derive the view projections. | Conditional callers, nested state, descriptors, callbacks and saved/live phases mean source spelling alone can't prove one live object or that every transition moved. |
| A declaration looks authoritative but consumers re-infer facts at runtime (separate #60 compiler/runtime cutover) | Ask whether a typed plan could derive the answers at the longest *correct* lifetime, with runtime consuming it instead of re-inferring. | The exact source transition keeps `.setdefault` and other OPEN behavior. Census the leftover runtime heuristics, source identity, evaluation phase and ABI before claiming parity or removal. This is separate from the CellProfiler family migration. |

Agent procedure:

1. **Select** a source/revision/import-root corpus. NRA or cheap literal/substring/name searches can rank candidate files.
2. **Census every original `ClassDef`** via the shared syntax index and join index-admitted class-family owners. Keep unmatched/conditional/nested rows OPEN.
3. **Overlay** residual decision sites.
4. **Name** candidate domain questions.
5. **Trace** answer provenance to each possible determining source.
6. **Separate** replicas from roles that legitimately vary on their own.
7. **Propose** a bounded `R*` and try to break it with a new case, an unknown input and counterexamples.
8. **Accept** it under task authority, or keep it OPEN.
9. **Express** the chosen move in NRA's existing staged DSL and validate architecture and behavior independently.

Heuristics give you recall. The agent proposes owners and relations from the evidence and checks them against the task rules or the specs/declarations the task points to. After that, NRA preflight/simulation gives mechanical leverage. It doesn't prove business meaning. Don't ask the user to do the trace.

## Agent's decision receipt (a thinking worksheet, not an NRA wire schema)

1. **Scope and role names:** exact checkout and source/dependency revisions, the task rules and specs/declarations, what we're looking at, domain noun(s), lifetime/phase, independent future variation and same-shape/different-meaning collisions.
2. **Source witnesses:** original declaration, method, branch, caller, fallback and consumer rows. Tell independently writable rosters from maps derived from them. Tell schema, domain, UI and transport authority apart. Keep unmatched, dynamic, alias, duplicate/rebound and OPEN rows, with the reason each is unresolved.
3. **Candidate `R*`:** candidate required and forbidden implementation–consumer/class pairs, the proposed determining authority *per fact family*, the public parent contract or alternative owner you'd need, derived projections, named exceptions and leftover boundaries. A subtype that doesn't exist is not by itself a missing obligation.
4. **Decision:** `propose`, `alternative` or `abstain`. Cite source evidence, say why independent maintenance shrinks, and name the counterevidence and the conditions for accepting. Cite the task rule or spec that licenses each required/forbidden pair you accept. The agent can derive the pairs that follow and propose alternatives. Meaning that's truly missing or contested stays OPEN until someone makes a domain decision. A judge's confidence, a historical diff or a repeated spelling doesn't establish that behavior is equivalent.

**Dispatch counterexample drill:** With `Format(JSON, XML, CSV)`, `Encoder.encode`, and aligned helpers, a missing `CsvEncoder` is only a question. `elif` is not source-spelled `else: if`. `inbound.kind` and `outbound.kind` are different subjects. A preceding urgent-priority `if` and a following `raise unknown(kind)` outside the lexical `else` stay ordered siblings. Duplicate methods/classes, decorators and defaults can change live binding. Source-syntax correlation can be useful without green-lighting any plan.
